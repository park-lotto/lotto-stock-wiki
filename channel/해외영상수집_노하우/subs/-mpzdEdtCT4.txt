Hello everyone. In this video, I'll
guide you through the complete
step-by-step process of creating a
verified Douyin account, which is the
official Chinese version of TikTok.
To get started, you cannot use the
standard App Store from Western regions,
so you need to head over to the official
Douyin website or change your App Store
region to China Mainland to download the
actual app.
Once you open the application, tap on
the profile icon located at the bottom
right corner to bring up the login
screen.
Next, click on the country code
drop-down menu and carefully scroll
through the list to find your specific
country code, which is usually listed
under the first letter of your country's
name in Chinese or simply by the plus
signs.
Enter your mobile phone number, tap the
box to agree to the terms of service,
and then press the large red button to
request your verification code.
Wait a moment for the text message to
arrive, enter the code quickly before
the timer expires, and complete any
security slider puzzle that pops up on
your screen.
Final step is to choose your username,
set a secure password, and your brand
new Douyin account is fully ready to
explore.
