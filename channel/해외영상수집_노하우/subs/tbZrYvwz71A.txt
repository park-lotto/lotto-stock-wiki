# Online Fanatic · 조회 51045 · 63초 · .en

in this video I will show you how to
download videos from rednote without any
Watermark first go to your rednote app
from there pick a video you want to
download then tap on the share button
you want to look for the copy link
icon tap on that to copy the
link then you want to open your Google
Chrome or Safari
on the search bar type in rednote
downloader tap on the first
option next paste your link here and
then tap on the download media
button wait for the processing to be
done and you can tap on the download
button this will be stored in your
downloads folder which you can access
and see that the video downloaded has no
watermark
