# How To Download Videos From Rednote App Without Watermark (Quick Guide)
# Knowledge Wisp · 조회 10375 · 62초 · .en

Here is how you can download videos from
RedNote app with no watermark. Open the
app and select the video you want to
download.
Then tap on the share button.
Tap on the copy link option.
After that, you want to go to Google
Chrome web browser or Safari for iOS
users. Open a brand new tab and type
RedNote downloader.
Select the first search result called
rednote-downloader.com.
Now you want to paste the copied RedNote
link here. You will see that the link
has titles as well. It will not prevent
the site from recognizing the video. Tap
on the download media button.
Once the video is validated, tap on the
download button.
Open the media file and you will see
that the video has been downloaded to
your phone with no watermark.
